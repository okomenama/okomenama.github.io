This project quantifies how parametric uncertainty in an Earth system model
propagates into risk assessments of climate tipping, using a
non-stationary-extreme-value framing rather than assuming a fixed climate
distribution. A central application is the weakening of the Atlantic
Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC), where parametric uncertainty
directly shapes the projected likelihood and timing of weakening.

Presented at EGU General Assembly 2026 (Vienna, Poster) and the JpGU-AGU
Joint Meeting 2026 (Makuhari, Oral). Related manuscript: Kubo, A., & Sawada,
Y. (2026), "Parametric Uncertainty in Prediction of AMOC Weakening by an
Earth System Model."
