The quantum lattice Boltzmann method (QLBM) is a quantum-computing analogue
of the classical Lattice Boltzmann method for fluid simulation. A key
difficulty is representing the nonlinear collision term that governs
diffusion, since quantum circuits naturally implement linear operations.
During this internship, a two-dimensional, linearly-approximated version of
the collision term was implemented and benchmarked for accuracy and
performance against the classical formulation.

Fujitsu Summer Research Internship, Sep. 1 – Oct. 3, 2025.
